# HR Analytics Dashboard 📊

A comprehensive Power BI dashboard for analyzing human resources metrics and workforce analytics across organizational departments.

## 🎯 Overview

This Power BI dashboard transforms raw HR data into actionable insights by analyzing workforce patterns across three critical departments: Research & Development, Human Resources, and Sales. The dashboard addresses key business challenges around employee retention, compensation effectiveness, and workforce optimization through interactive visualizations and data-driven storytelling.

## 📈 Key Features & Analysis

### 🔍 **Multi-Department Comparative Analysis**
- **Research & Development**: 961 employees with 13.8% attrition rate - maintains the most stable workforce indicating effective retention strategies in technical roles
- **Human Resources**: 63 employees with 19.0% attrition rate - concerning internal retention issues despite being responsible for company-wide employee engagement
- **Sales**: 446 employees with 20.6% attrition rate - highest turnover creates revenue risk and indicates challenges in high-pressure sales environment

### 📊 **Comprehensive Workforce Metrics**
- **Employee Demographics**: Average age ranges 37-38 years across departments with tenure averaging 6.9-7.3 years, showing mature workforce with reasonable retention
- **Salary Analysis**: Average salaries between 6.3K-7.0K reveal compensation competitiveness varies by department
- **Attrition Tracking**: Real-time monitoring of departures enables proactive intervention strategies
- **Gender Distribution**: Visual representation shows male-dominated departures requiring targeted retention approaches

### 🎨 **Interactive Visualization Techniques**
- **Donut Charts**: Educational background analysis reveals 79% of R&D has Life Sciences/Medical qualifications, showing specialized skill concentration
- **Bar Charts**: Salary band visualization consistently shows employees earning ≤5K represent 80%+ of attrition across all departments
- **Line Graphs**: Tenure analysis demonstrates highest risk period is 0-2 years, indicating onboarding and early engagement challenges
- **Heat Maps**: Gender-based patterns reveal male employees have 2-3x higher departure rates, suggesting targeted retention needs

## 🔎 Detailed Insights & Analysis

### 🚨 **Critical Risk Factors Identified**
- **Age Demographics**: 26-35 year age group dominates attrition (67 in R&D, 8 in HR, 41 in Sales) indicating mid-career transition challenges and advancement opportunity gaps
- **Compensation Impact**: Lower salary brackets (≤5K) account for 83% of R&D, 83% of HR, and 47% of Sales attrition, highlighting urgent need for compensation review
- **Gender Patterns**: Male attrition rates significantly higher (88/133 in R&D, 47/92 in Sales) suggesting work-life balance or career development issues
- **Early Tenure Risk**: Peak attrition occurs within first 2 years across all departments, indicating onboarding process improvements needed

### 📋 **Department-Specific Analysis**

#### Research & Development - Technical Talent Retention
- **Role Concentration**: Laboratory Technicians represent 47% of all departures (62/133), indicating role-specific dissatisfaction or limited career progression
- **Educational Specialization**: 44% Life Sciences + 35% Medical backgrounds create narrow talent pipeline requiring specialized retention strategies
- **Stability Factors**: Lowest overall attrition rate suggests effective long-term retention but challenges in specific technical roles
- **Risk Management**: Need for targeted programs addressing Laboratory Technician retention and career development pathways

#### Human Resources - Internal Practice Challenges  
- **Compensation Crisis**: 100% of attrition occurs in lowest salary bracket (≤5K), indicating internal pay equity issues within HR department
- **Professional Development**: 58% Human Resources education background suggests limited cross-functional experience may restrict career options
- **Leadership Concern**: High attrition rate in department responsible for retention strategy indicates systemic issues requiring immediate attention  
- **Age Concentration**: 67% of departures in 26-35 age group suggests mid-level HR professionals seeking external opportunities

#### Sales - Revenue-Critical Retention Issues
- **Executive Flight Risk**: Sales Executives account for 62% of departures (57/92), creating direct revenue impact and client relationship disruption
- **Performance Pressure**: Highest departmental attrition rate indicates high-stress environment may require culture and support system improvements
- **Educational Diversity**: Marketing (38%) and Life Sciences (32%) backgrounds show diverse recruitment but retention challenges persist
- **Compensation Spread**: Attrition across multiple salary bands (47% in ≤5K, 39% in 5K-10K) suggests comprehensive compensation restructuring needed
